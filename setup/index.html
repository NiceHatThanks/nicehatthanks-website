01 · GET SCOOPY ONLINE

First, connect Scoopy to your Wi-Fi.

When Scoopy has no saved Wi-Fi network, it automatically creates its own temporary setup network.

01

Plug Scoopy into USB power

Use a USB-C cable and any normal USB power source. Scoopy will boot automatically.

If it has not been connected to Wi-Fi before, the middle red LED will gently pulse. That means Scoopy is ready for setup.

[IMAGE: Scoopy red LED pulsing]Replace with a product photo or short animation later.
02

Join the Scoopy setup Wi-Fi

On your phone, tablet or computer, open Wi-Fi settings and look for a network named scoopy-XXXXXX.

The final six characters are unique to your Scoopy, so your network name will look something like scoopy-A1B2C3.

Wi-Fi password: scoopy123
[IMAGE: Phone Wi-Fi menu showing scoopy-XXXXXX]Replace with an iPhone, Android or laptop Wi-Fi screenshot.
03

Open the Scoopy setup page

The captive portal should open automatically after you connect to the Scoopy Wi-Fi network.

If nothing appears, open a browser and go to http://192.168.4.1/.

[IMAGE: Scoopy captive portal]Replace with the real ESPHome captive portal screenshot.
04

Choose your home Wi-Fi

Scoopy will show the nearby Wi-Fi networks it can see. Select your home network, enter its password, then press Connect Scoopy.

Scoopy will save those details and connect to your network.

05

Wait for the red LED to turn off

Once the Wi-Fi connection succeeds, the setup page should normally close by itself and your phone or computer can return to its usual Wi-Fi network.

The middle red setup LED turns off when Scoopy is connected.

That’s the Wi-Fi part done. Scoopy is now on your local network and ready for Home Assistant.

WI-FI CHECK

If the setup page doesn’t appear.

Captive portals can behave slightly differently between phones and computers. These three checks fix most setup hiccups.

Check you joined Scoopy

Make sure your device is connected to scoopy-XXXXXX, not your normal home Wi-Fi.

Open it manually

Visit http://192.168.4.1/ in your browser while you are still connected to Scoopy.

Ignore “no internet”

The Scoopy setup network is local only, so your phone may warn that it has no internet connection. That is expected during setup.

02 · ADD IT TO HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant should find Scoopy for you.

Once Scoopy is on your home Wi-Fi, ESPHome discovery should make it appear in Home Assistant automatically.

06

Open Home Assistant

Look for a newly discovered ESPHome device. Depending on your Home Assistant setup, this may appear as a discovery notification or under Settings → Devices & services.

[IMAGE: Home Assistant discovery screen]Replace with the final Home Assistant discovery screenshot.
07

Add Scoopy

Select the discovered Scoopy device and follow Home Assistant’s prompts to add it.

Once added, you can use its buttons, status LEDs and — on the Presence version — presence sensor in your automations and dashboards.

08

Make it yours

Try something simple first: make a button toggle a light, use an LED to show whether the front door is locked, or trigger a favourite Home Assistant scene.

After that, Scoopy is just another local Home Assistant device — use it however you like.

MOVING HOUSE? CHANGING WI-FI?

Reset Scoopy’s Wi-Fi and start again.

You do not need to reflash anything. Scoopy can forget its saved Wi-Fi details and return to setup mode using the two physical buttons.

01

Hold both buttons

Press and hold both physical buttons together for 10 seconds.

02

Watch for the warning

During the final part of the hold, the middle red LED flashes quickly to warn that a reset is about to happen.

03

Set it up again

Scoopy clears its Wi-Fi credentials, the red LED begins pulsing again, and the scoopy-XXXXXX setup network returns.

A SMALL TECHNICAL NOTE

Setup stays local.

Scoopy uses ESPHome’s local Wi-Fi setup flow. Your Wi-Fi details are sent directly to the Scoopy you are connected to — there is no Scoopy cloud account involved in onboarding.
